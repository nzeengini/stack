01. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how NZEENGINI ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in Kavutse (the "App"), an offline number sequence puzzle game. The App challenges players to identify mathematical patterns, choose answers, use hints, and progress through levels.

We designed Kavutse to work without an account or online service. This policy applies to the App as distributed by NZEENGINI.

02. Information and local storage

We do not collect personal information through Kavutse. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or other identifying details.

The App may store the following on your device to support gameplay:

  • High scores, game progress, streaks, and related game statistics.
  • Difficulty selections and other game settings or preferences.

These values are stored locally using .NET MAUI Preferences or equivalent on-device storage. They are not sent to NZEENGINI servers. Kavutse does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or developer-controlled telemetry.

Important device-level distinction

Your operating system may manage device backups or app diagnostics independently of Kavutse. Depending on your device settings, local app preferences may be included in an operating-system backup. NZEENGINI does not operate or access those backup services.

03. Internet access and permissions

Kavutse is intended to be fully playable offline. Gameplay does not require internet connectivity, registration, sign-in, or access to an external server.

The App is designed not to request sensitive or runtime permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files. It does not need these features to function.

Some operating-system or app-store components may have their own permissions or connectivity behavior outside the App's control. This policy describes the App's own functionality.

04. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the App because the App does not collect it. Kavutse is designed without advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, social login, or in-app tracking services.

If you choose to email us, your email provider and ours will process that communication according to their respective privacy practices. Email correspondence is separate from in-app gameplay.

05. Age suitability and children's privacy

Kavutse is intended for people aged 13 and above. The App does not require personal information from players of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

Age suitability is a description of the intended audience, not a substitute for the age ratings or target-audience declarations required by Google Play. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact us.

06. Your choices and controls

Since we do not maintain player accounts or a server-side player database, there is no account information to request, export, or delete from us.

You can manage local game information through any reset option provided in the App, or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local data, subject to any backups retained by your operating system.

You can play without providing personal information to NZEENGINI.

07. Security and data retention

Keeping gameplay data on the device minimizes exposure to remote data collection. The App relies on the operating system's application storage protections for locally stored preferences and scores.

Local game information remains on the device until you reset it, clear application storage, or uninstall the App, subject to the operating system's backup and restore behavior. We do not set a server-side retention period because we do not receive this game data.

08. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Kavutse's features, data practices, or legal obligations change. Any updated version will display a revised effective date and will be made available through the App's public privacy policy page. Material changes to data handling should be reflected in the App and its Google Play disclosures before they take effect.
