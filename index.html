Our story

Rooted in community.
Driven by possibility.

NZEENGINI is the innovation and digital skills department of Bethany Rehabilitation Center in Kisoro, Uganda.

Our mission is to uplift young people in surrounding villages through life-support skills, technology and economic empowerment — nurturing their potential to make a lasting difference where they live.
